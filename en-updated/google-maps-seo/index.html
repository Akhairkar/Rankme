Google Maps SEO

Why your business isn't showing up on Google Maps

If customers say they "can't find you on Maps," it's usually one of a handful of common causes — not a mystery algorithm problem. Here's how to think about it.

How Google Maps ranking actually works

Google has stated that Maps results are influenced by three broad factors: relevance (how well your profile matches what someone searched for), distance (how close you are to the searcher or the area they searched), and prominence (how well-known your business is, based on signals like reviews and information found across the web).

No one outside Google controls the exact weighting of these factors, and no legitimate service can guarantee a specific position. What you can control is making each of these three factors as strong as possible for your own listing.

We won't claim to guarantee your ranking — anyone who does isn't being straight with you. What we can help with is making sure nothing basic is holding your profile back.

Common reasons businesses don't show up

  • Unverified or suspended profile: an unverified listing may not appear at all, and policy violations (like a fake address) can get a listing suspended.
  • Wrong or too-broad category: if your primary category doesn't match how customers search, you may simply not be eligible to show for those searches.
  • Weak or inconsistent business information: mismatched name/address/phone details across the web (your website, directories, social pages) can weaken prominence signals.
  • Few or no reviews: review count and rating are part of prominence — a profile with zero reviews often struggles against nearby competitors that have several.
  • Searching from far away: distance matters — checking your own ranking from another city or a spoofed location gives misleading results.

What to check first

  1. Confirm your profile is verified and in good standing (no policy warnings)
  2. Double-check your category matches your actual business
  3. Make sure your name, address and phone number match exactly across your website and any directories you're listed on
  4. Look at how many reviews similar nearby businesses have, for a realistic comparison
