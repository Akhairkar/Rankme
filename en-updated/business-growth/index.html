Getting customers from Google: where to start

"Get more customers from Google" isn't one task — it's a few pieces working together. Here's how they connect, and a sensible order to tackle them in.

The pieces that work together

Four things determine most of a local business's visibility and conversion from Google: your Business Profile (do you show up, and does it look complete), your reviews (do people trust what they see), your local SEO basics (is your information consistent everywhere), and your website (does it actually convert a visitor into a customer once they arrive).

Most businesses fix these one piece at a time, usually in the order below — not because it's a strict rule, but because each step makes the next one more effective.

A sensible order to work through

  1. Get your Google Business Profile right first. It's free, it's usually the first thing customers see, and other fixes matter less if this is broken.
  2. Build a steady flow of genuine reviews. This strengthens both trust and your Maps prominence.
  3. Check your local SEO consistency. Make sure your business details match across your website and any directories.
  4. Improve your website's basics. Mobile-friendliness, clear contact info, and fast load times matter once people start clicking through.
  5. Consider ongoing monitoring. Profiles and listings drift over time — periodic checks catch problems early.
There's no shortcut that replaces getting the basics right. Paid ads or promotions can add visibility on top of this foundation, but they don't fix a Business Profile with wrong hours or a website that doesn't load properly on a phone.

What "growth" realistically looks like

Improvements from getting these basics right tend to show up gradually — more profile views, more calls or direction requests, and slowly more reviews — rather than as an overnight jump. Be wary of anyone promising fast, guaranteed results; that's not how Google's local ecosystem works.
