Compliance & Policy

Google Policy Compliance & Safety Center

Last updated: 30 October 2026
In plain language: We connect to Google only through official APIs after you say yes. We read what we need, show it back to you, and do not sell it or use it for ads. We do not scrape Google, write or buy fake reviews, or filter who gets asked for a review. We cannot promise rankings. This page describes our own practices; it is not a certificate or approval from Google.

1. How we work with Google's policies

LocalBoost is an independent tool. We design our features to follow the policies that apply to products built on Google services, including the Google Business Profile policies, the Google API Services User Data Policy and the review and user-generated-content rules. Policies change, so we review features when Google updates them. This page describes our practices; it does not mean Google has reviewed, certified or approved LocalBoost.

2. Official APIs and your consent only

We access Google Business Profile and Search Console data only through Google's official APIs using OAuth 2.0, and only after you grant permission on Google's consent screen. Search Console access is requested separately and is read-only. We request the access each feature needs and no more. We do not scrape Google Maps, Google Search or any other Google property.

3. Limited Use of Google user data

Data received from Google is used only to provide and improve user-facing features that you request within LocalBoost. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, give it to data brokers, or use it to train generalised AI models, and we restrict human access to it as described in our Privacy Policy.

LocalBoost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Reviews: no fake, incentivised or gated reviews

  • No fake reviews. We never write, sell, broker or simulate reviews or reviewer accounts.
  • No review gating. Our review request tools send every customer to the same Google review link. We do not offer a route that screens out unhappy customers.
  • No incentives. We advise against offering money, discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews.
  • You approve replies. Review replies, including any AI-drafted ones, are posted to Google only after you confirm them.

5. No guaranteed rankings

Google decides how businesses are ranked. We make no promise of any position, keyword placement or traffic volume. Our rank views are estimates from the data we can collect and are labelled as such; where data is not available we show it as unavailable.

6. You stay in control

You own your Google Business Profile. You can disconnect Google from your account page, which revokes the token we hold, and you can separately remove LocalBoost's access from your Google Account's third-party access settings. See Data Retention & Deletion for export and deletion.

7. No advertising

LocalBoost does not run advertising and does not use Google data for ad targeting.

8. Report a concern

If you think LocalBoost is being misused or a page is inaccurate, write to support@localboost.in. We read the Disclaimer and Terms as part of the same commitments.
