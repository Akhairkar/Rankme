What we store in your browser

Most of what we keep is browser storage rather than cookies. Browser storage stays on your device and is not sent to us with every request. The table lists everything the website code writes today.

The waitlist and contact forms store nothing in your browser. What you type is sent only when you press the button, and the waitlist falls back to an email link when the service is not connected.

Analytics

Analytics are first-party and run only if you press Accept. The analytics script is not even downloaded until you accept. If you decline, or do nothing, no analytics events are sent. When you accept and the API is connected, the script sends a small event to our own API for a fixed list of events: page view, tool used, audit started, audit completed, sign-up started, Google connect clicked, waitlist submitted, pricing viewed, and checkout started or completed. Each event carries only the page path (without query string or fragment), the language and, where relevant, the tool or plan name. It sends no cookie, no user identifier and no free text. The server stores no IP address or user agent with the event. To count unique visitors it uses a daily hash that changes each day, so people cannot be followed across days. Raw events are deleted after 90 days and kept only as daily totals.

You can change your mind at any time through the Cookie settings link in the footer, or by clearing this site's local storage.

What we do not use

  • No advertising cookies, advertising scripts or tracking pixels.
  • No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • We do not claim to implement Google Consent Mode and do not send consent signals to Google advertising or analytics products, because we use none of them.

Your choices

  • Use Cookie settings in the footer to accept or decline analytics.
  • Use your browser settings to block or clear cookies and site data. The free tools still work, but preferences such as your theme and checklist ticks will not be remembered.
  • If you disable cookies, you will not be able to stay signed in once sign-in is available.

Changes and questions

If we add storage or a third party, we update this page and the date above before it goes live, and the changelog will note it. Questions go to support@localboost.in. See also the privacy FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

Do you use advertising cookies?
No. There are no advertising cookies, scripts or pixels on LocalBoost.
Is analytics on by default?
No. Analytics are sent only after you press Accept on the cookie banner.
Do you support Google Consent Mode?
We do not claim to. We use no Google advertising or analytics products, so there is nothing to signal.
