Our Mission & Methodology

Empowering Indian Local Businesses on Google Maps

LocalBoost gives small Indian businesses clear, honest local SEO guidance: free tools you can use today, and a connected product (Pro) that is launching soon. We aim to show what is wrong, what to fix first, and the evidence behind it, without ranking promises.

The problem

Local SEO advice is often vague, expensive or too good to be true

Shop owners, clinics, restaurants and home-service providers are regularly pitched packages that promise “#1 on Google”, sometimes using tactics that break Google’s rules, such as fake reviews, and that can get a profile suspended. Honest guidance is hard to find, and reports often show scores without saying where they came from.

LocalBoost is our answer: policy-compliant checklists and tools, written for Indian businesses, and reports that show their evidence.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Real data only

If we have not measured something, we say so. A missing number reads “awaiting data”, never an invented one.

Transparent reports

Every finding shows what we saw, the source and why it matters. Simulated and estimated values are labelled.

India first

Guides and tools are written for Indian businesses, cities and languages, including Hindi, and for how customers here actually find shops and services.

No ranking guarantees

Local rankings depend on relevance, distance, prominence and competition. We never promise a position, and we do not support fake reviews or other tactics against Google’s policies.
