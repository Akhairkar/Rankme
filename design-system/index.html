Chart tokens and SVG helpers

Categorical --chart-1..8 (each 3:1 on surface), sequential --chart-seq-1..5, semantic --chart-good/mid/bad. Always add direct labels or a table; do not rely on colour alone. Charts below use illustrative sample values.

App shell (dashboard)

Under 1024px the sidebar is an off-canvas drawer (toggle with data-lb-nav-toggle, needs lb-ui.js); from 1024px it is a sticky column. No horizontal page scroll: wrap tables in .lb-table-wrap.
