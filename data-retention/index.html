Data Retention

Data Retention & Deletion

Last updated: 30 October 2026
In plain language: We keep your data while your account is active. You can download it as JSON, disconnect Google (which revokes our token), or delete your account from the account page. Deleting removes your businesses, keywords, reviews, audits and Google connection from our live database. We keep payment and tax records for as long as the law requires, and we keep waitlist and contact entries only as long as needed to reply.

1. Why retention matters

You should be able to see what we hold, take a copy and have it removed. This page explains each category of data in LocalBoost, how long we keep it, and what you can do yourself. It complements the Privacy Policy. Retention periods below are our policy; where a law requires a record to be kept, the legal requirement prevails.

2. What we keep and for how long

DataHow long
Account details (name, email)While your account is active; removed on deletion.
Businesses, keywords, competitors, audit and rank-scan resultsWhile your account is active; removed on deletion, or when you archive or delete the item.
Google Business Profile, review and Search Console dataWhile connected and your account is active; removed when you delete the account. Disconnecting stops new data being read.
Google access (refresh) tokenUntil you disconnect or delete the account; revoked with Google at that time.
Sign-in sessionsUntil expiry or sign-out; expired sessions are cleared.
Payment, order and invoice recordsAs long as tax, accounting and other laws require, even after account deletion. Card details are never stored by us.
Waitlist and contact submissionsOnly as long as needed to reply or to tell you about launches; removed on request.
Consent-based analytics eventsProduct-usage events with no IP address; kept only as long as useful for improving the product.
Rate-limit countersShort-lived, cleaned up automatically.

3. Export your data

Signed-in users can download a copy of everything stored against their account from the account page. The download is a JSON file generated by the export endpoint (/api/account/export) and includes the businesses, keywords, results and records that belong to you. Some screens may also let you download audit or rank results as CSV. If you cannot sign in, email us from your registered address and we will help after verifying it is you.

4. Disconnect Google

On the account page you can disconnect Google at any time. This revokes the token with Google and deletes the saved connection. Data already imported stays until you delete it or the account, so you can keep earlier reports. You can also open your Google Account's security settings and remove LocalBoost from third-party access; the next time we try to use that access it will fail and we will treat the connection as removed.

5. Delete your account

Use the delete option on the account page. We ask you to confirm because it cannot be undone. Deletion removes your account, businesses, keywords, competitors, reviews, audits and Google connections from our live database. We keep only what the law requires, mainly payment and invoice records, and these are no longer used for anything else. Deleting the account does not cancel anything you bought elsewhere, so cancel the plan first as described in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. Backups and providers

Our infrastructure provider may keep database backups or logs for a limited period after deletion, and these age out on the provider's schedule. We do not use them to restore a deleted account unless there has been a technical fault. Payment providers keep their own records under their own policies, and data you have already published to Google remains on Google.

7. Waitlist, contact and email

If you joined the waitlist or wrote to us without making an account, email support@localboost.in from the address you used and ask for removal. We will confirm once it is done. We will list email providers on the Privacy Policy when email sending is enabled.

8. Analytics choices

Analytics events are sent only if you accept the cookie banner, and they do not store your IP address. If you declined or want to change your choice, clear this site's local storage in your browser and the banner will ask again.

9. Requests and grievances

For any access, correction, erasure or complaint request, write to support@localboost.in. We may need to verify your identity first. Grievance contact: support@localboost.in. See also the Terms of Service and the Google compliance page.
