Frequently Asked Questions

How important are Google peak hours for gyms?

Very important. Prospective members check the 'Popular Times' graph on Google Maps to avoid overcrowding. Ensure your business hours reflect separate morning and evening shifts.

Should gyms post membership fees on Google?

Listing starting membership rates (e.g. 1-Month, 3-Month, Annual) in the 'Services' tab establishes transparency and drives qualified leads who are ready to pay.

When is the best time to ask gym members for reviews?

Ask members when they achieve a personal fitness milestone (e.g. after their 1st month of consistency, or noticeable weight loss). Their enthusiasm produces detailed, heartfelt testimonials.
