Industry Playbook

Local SEO & Google Maps Guide for Interior Designers & Architects

Attract high-ticket residential flat renovations and commercial office design clients with optimized local 3-pack visibility.

1. Primary & Secondary Categories on Google

Primary Category
Interior Designer
This should be the single most accurate definition of your business core operations.

Recommended Secondary Categories: Architect, Home Builder, Kitchen Remodeler.

2. Profile Configuration & Local Attributes

High-Resolution 3D & Real Site Portfolio: Regularly upload before-and-after renovation photos, modular kitchen designs, and false ceiling lighting.

3. Essential Services & Menu Catalog Structure

Comprehensive Service Tiers: Detail 2BHK/3BHK turnkey interior design, modular wardrobe fabrication, commercial office fit-outs, and 3D elevation drawings.

4. Photo Guidelines & Visual Trust Signals

Video Walkthrough Tours: Post 30-second reels and client handover testimonial videos to boost Google profile dwell time.

5. Compliant Review Acquisition & High-Intent Keywords

Architectural Schema on Website: Implement `LocalBusiness` and `Project` schemas with localized project galleries linking back to your Google Maps pin.

Client Testimonials on Execution: Encourage homeowners to mention specific room transformations (e.g. 'renovated my 3BHK in Gurgaon on time').

6. Choosing categories honestly

"Interior designer" is the usual primary category. Add "Architect" only if you are a registered architect, and "Furniture maker" or "Modular kitchen supplier" only if you actually manufacture or sell those. Your category also sets expectations: a client who wants a full-home renovation behaves differently from one who wants only a wardrobe.

7. Services and project types to list

  • Full-home interiors for 1BHK, 2BHK and 3BHK flats
  • Modular kitchens, wardrobes and TV units
  • Office and shop fit-outs
  • Renovation, false ceilings and lighting design
  • 3D design and walk-through visualisation
  • Turnkey projects with civil, electrical and carpentry included

For each, say what is included, the cities or localities you cover and how a consultation works. Giving a rough starting range per square foot is more helpful than hiding all pricing, provided you explain what affects it.

8. Portfolio photos on your profile

Upload finished projects, ideally with before-and-after pairs, taken in daylight after the site is cleaned. Photograph rooms from a corner to show space, and add the project type in the caption. Always have the client's permission, and never use images of another designer's work.

9. Review requests that comply with policy

Interior projects last weeks, so ask at handover when the client sees the finished home, and again after a month. Send the same review link to every client by WhatsApp. Do not offer a free upgrade or discount in return, and do not pass off a client testimonial from a brochure as a Google review.

10. Questions clients ask

How long will a 2BHK take? Do you handle civil work? Is there a warranty on carpentry? Can I see an ongoing site? How is payment staged? Put the honest answers in your services text and on your website.

11. Seasonality

Enquiries often rise after possession of a new flat, before festivals like Diwali when families want a finished home, and ahead of wedding functions. Monsoon months can slow site work, so be clear about timelines then.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do interior designers get leads from Google Maps or only Instagram?

Google Maps generates the highest-intent clients ready to hire immediately, whereas social media primarily generates visual browsing.

What is the best primary category for a turnkey interior firm?

Choose 'Interior Designer' as primary, and add 'Architect' and 'Kitchen Remodeler' as secondary categories.
