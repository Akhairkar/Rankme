Frequently Asked Questions

What legal details should real estate agents include on Google?

Under RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority), consultants should state their official RERA registration number prominently in their Google Business description.

How can property brokers get reviews from busy buyers?

Send a congratulations message on the day of registry or key handover alongside a direct 1-click Google review link.
