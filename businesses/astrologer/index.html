Local SEO & Google Maps Guide for Astrologers & Vastu Consultants

How astrologers, numerologists, and Vastu consultants achieve top rankings in Google's Local 3-Pack and attract client consultations.

1. Primary & Secondary Categories on Google

Primary Category
Astrologer
This should be the single most accurate definition of your business core operations.

Recommended Secondary Categories: Vastu Consultant, Numerologist, Psychic.

2. Profile Configuration & Local Attributes

Confidential Consultation Booking Attributes: Setup WhatsApp chat appointment links allowing private inquiries for Kundli matching and birth chart reading.

3. Essential Services & Menu Catalog Structure

Service Structuring: Clearly list separate services for Horoscope Reading, Gemstone Consultation, Vastu Dosha Remedy, and Palmistry with consultation fees.

4. Photo Guidelines & Visual Trust Signals

Local Trust & Ethics: Strict compliance with Google guidelines prohibiting false medical cure claims or guaranteed fate outcomes.

5. Compliant Review Acquisition & High-Intent Keywords

High-Intent Bilingual Keywords: Rank for 'best astrologer near me', 'vastu consultant for new flat', 'kundli milan specialist', and 'jyotish paramarsh'.

Photo Guidelines: Professional consultation chamber photo, sacred geometric Vastu yantras, certificated credentials, and welcoming reception ambience.

6. Which categories to choose and which to avoid

Choose the category that matches how clients describe you. "Astrologer" suits most jyotish practices; add "Vastu consultant" or "Numerologist" only if you really offer those services. Avoid categories that suggest unrelated trades, such as jewellery shop, unless you run one. If you sell gemstones or rudraksha from a shop, that is a separate retail activity and may deserve its own listing or clear service labelling.

7. Services to list, with plain descriptions

Write each service in the words clients use, and state how it is delivered:

  • Kundli reading and birth chart analysis (in person or by video call)
  • Kundli matching for marriage (Kundli milan)
  • Muhurat consultation for weddings, griha pravesh and business openings
  • Vastu consultation for homes, shops and offices (on-site or by plan)
  • Numerology and name-spelling guidance
  • Languages you consult in: Hindi, English, Marathi, Gujarati, Tamil and so on

Mention your consultation fee or a fee range and the usual duration, so people do not have to ask on the first call.

8. Photos that build trust

Show your consultation room, your seating, your entrance and signboard, and framed qualifications or association membership if you hold them. Photos of clients' faces or charts should never be published without clear permission. A calm, clean, well-lit room tells a first-time visitor what to expect.

9. Reviews without breaking Google policy or client privacy

Ask every client, not only the satisfied ones, and share the plain review link by WhatsApp after the session. Do not offer a discount, a free reading or a puja for a review. Suggest that people comment on punctuality, clarity and respect, not on predictions or personal details. Reply to reviews briefly and never repeat anything private from a client's chart.

10. Questions customers actually ask

People search before they call. Expect questions such as: do you take appointments only, can I consult online, what is the fee, do you speak my language, how long does a session take, and is Vastu possible for a rented flat. Answer these in your description, services and FAQ section.

11. Seasonality

Demand rises around wedding seasons for Kundli matching and muhurat, before Navratri and Diwali for business and griha pravesh queries, and at the start of the new year for annual readings. Plan photos, posts and extra availability a few weeks before these periods.

12. A note on claims

Google's policies and Indian advertising rules discourage claims that promise cures, guaranteed results or control over fate. Describe what you do (analysis, guidance, remedies you recommend) rather than what you guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can astrologers list telephone consultation as a service area?

Yes, but Google requires a physical office address for verification. Once verified, you can specify your service radius.

How to ask for reviews without compromising client privacy?

Ask clients to comment on your guidance professionalism, punctuality, and depth of explanation rather than sharing private birth chart details.
