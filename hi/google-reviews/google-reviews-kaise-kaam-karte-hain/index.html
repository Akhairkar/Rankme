Google Reviews Kaise Kaam Karte Hain: Local Ranking Algorithm Ki Puri Jankari

Aisa kyu hota hai ki 40 reviews wali dukaan 200 reviews wale competitor se upar dikhti hai? Aaiye samjhein Google ka review algorithm asal mein kaise kaam karta hai.

6 Mukhya Baatein Jo Google Reviews Ki Ranking Tay Karti Hain

Google Local 3-Pack (Maps par top 3 dukaanein) mein rank karne ke liye customer reviews sabse mahatvapurna signals mein se ek hain. Lekin Google sirf total stars nahi ginta, balki NLP (Natural Language Processing) ke zariye review ke har shabd ko samajhta hai.

Samasya 1: Reviews Ki Sankhya Banam Taazgi (Recency)

Kam reviews wala business zyada reviews wale se aage kyu nikal jata hai?

Agar kisi business ke paas pichhle 3 mahine mein lagatar 45 naye reviews aaye hain, toh woh aksar us competitor se upar rank karega jiske paas 250 puraane reviews hain par saal bhar se koi naya review nahi aaya. Google Review Velocity (naye reviews aane ki raftaar) aur Review Recency ko behad ahmiyat deta hai.

Samasya 2: Review Ke Andar Service Ke Keywords

Customer ke shabdon se Maps ranking kaise badhti hai?

Jab customer review likhte samay service ka naam likhta hai (jaise 'best wedding photographer' ya 'fast screen repair'), toh Google use 'Review Justification' ke roop mein highlight karta hai. Jab koi search karega, toh Google aapki profile ko un reviews ke aadhar par top par dikhayega.

Samasya 3: Bayesian Star Rating Ka Ganit

Ek 1-star review pure average ko itna kyu gira deta hai?

Google sadharan average nahi nikalta. Woh review likhne wale ki credibility bhi dekhta hai. Ek active Local Guide ka 1-star review kisi naye, bina photo wale account ke 5-star review se zyada wazan rakhta hai.

Samasya 4: Google Ka Spam Filter Aur Gayab Hote Reviews

Asli customer ka review bhi kabhi-kabhi kyu nahi dikhta?

Google ke automated filters tab review block kar dete hain jab: ek hi Wi-Fi ya IP address se kai reviews daale jayein, review likhne wala vyakti kabhi us ilake mein gaya hi na ho, ya achanak ek din mein 20 reviews aa jayein. Isliye dukan par baithe customer se turant apne Wi-Fi par review na karwayein.

Samasya 5: Owner Ke Reply Ka Asar

Kya reviews ka jawab dene se ranking badhti hai?

Google ne aadhikarik taur par kaha hai ki reviews ka reply dene se customer ka vishwas badhta hai aur Google samajhta hai ki business active hai. 90% se zyada reply rate wali profiles ko Maps par zyada calls aur directions milte hain.

Samasya 6: Kanooni Niyam Aur Fake Reviews Par Rok

Consumer Protection Rules ke antargat kanoon

Bharat ke Consumer Protection Rules ke tehat paise dekar jhoothe reviews likhwana ya fake ratings kharidna kanoonan galat hai. Google aisi profiles ko turant permanently suspend kar deta hai.
