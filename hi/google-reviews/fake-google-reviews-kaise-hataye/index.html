Fake Google Reviews Kaise Hatayein: Official Google Process Ki Puri Jankari

Kya kisi competitor ya troll ne jhootha 1-star review daal diya hai? Aaiye samjhein Google ki policy ke tahat fake reviews ko officially kaise hatwaya jata hai.

Jhoothe Aur Fake Google Reviews Ko Hatane Ka Official Process

Google kisi review ko sirf isliye nahi hatata kyuki aapko customer ki baat pasand nahi aayi. Lekin agar koi review Google ki 'Prohibited Content' policy (jaise competitor ka attack, gaali-galoch, ya fake identity) ka ullanghan karta hai, toh Google use zaroor delete karta hai.

Samasya 1: Kaise Pehchanein Ki Review Fake Hai?

Kaunse reviews Google ki policy ke khilaf hain?

  • Fake Engagement: Review likhne wala vyakti kabhi dukan aaya hi na ho aur competitor ne fake account se daala ho.
  • Purane Employee Ka Badla: Kisi purane worker ne naukri chhutne ke gusse mein review likha ho.
  • Gaali-galoch Ya Dhamki: Review mein apashabd ya kisi staff member par niji hamla kiya gaya ho.
  • Bina Baat Ka Vivad: Dukan ki service ke bajaye rajneeti ya kisi anya vishay par likha ho.
Samasya 2: Google Maps Par Review Report (Flag) Karna

Kadam 1: Standard Flagging Ka Tarika

  1. Google Maps par apni dukan search karein.
  2. Kharab review par jayein aur bagal mein bane 3 dots (⋮) par click karein.
  3. 'Report review' chunein.
  4. Sahi reason chunein (jaise 'Spam or fake' ya 'Conflict of interest').
Samasya 3: Google Reviews Management Tool Ka Istemal

Kadam 2: Status check karein aur Appeal file karein

Google ka ek official tool hai jiska naam hai Google Business Profile Reviews Management Tool. Wahan jakar aap apne report kiye huye reviews ka live status dekh sakte hain. Agar pehli baar mein report reject ho jaye, toh aap wahan 'Appeal' button daba kar saboot upload kar sakte hain.

Samasya 4: Google Community Forum Par Escalate Karna

Kadam 3: Jab automated appeal reject ho jaye

Agar automated system review na hataye, toh Google Business Profile ke official Community Forum par post karein. Wahan Platinum Product Experts hote hain jo case ko seedhe Google ki Trust & Safety team tak pahunchate hain.

Samasya 5: Google Kaunse Reviews NAHI Hatata?

Review delete na hone ki sabse aam wajah

Agar customer ne mehnga hone ki shikayat ki hai, ya khane ka swaad pasand na aane par 1-star diya hai, toh Google use nahi hatata kyuki yeh customer ki niji raye mani jati hai. Iska ek hi hal hai: vinamra reply likhein.

Samasya 6: Indian Cyber Law Aur Kanooni Rasta

Defamation aur blackmailing ke khilaf action

Agar koi competitor ya vyakti paise maang raha ho aur review bombing kar raha ho, toh yeh Indian Penal Code ke tehat extortion aur defamation hai. Aap cybercrime.gov.in par complaint darj kar sakte hain aur Google Legal ko notice bhej sakte hain.

Saboot Zaroor Rakhein: Review ko report karne se pehle screenshot zaroor le lein. Agar koi WhatsApp par blackmail kar raha hai toh uski chat ka proof Google appeal mein attach karein.
