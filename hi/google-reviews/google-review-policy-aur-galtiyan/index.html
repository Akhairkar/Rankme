Google Review Policies: Yeh 6 Galtiyan Dukan Ki Listing Suspend Kara Sakti Hain

Bohot se business owners anjaane mein aisi galtiyan kar baithte hain jisse unke saare reviews delete ho jaate hain ya Google profile band ho jaati hai. Jaaniye kin galtiyon se bachna hai.

6 Khatarnak Galtiyan Jo Google Business Profile Ko Barbaad Kar Sakti Hain

Google ke automated AI filters ab bohot smart ho chuke hain. Agar aap koi short-cut ya jhootha tarika apnayenge, toh Google bina warning ke reviews delete kar dega ya account suspend kar dega.

Galti 1: Review Ke Badle Discount Ya Gift Dena

'5-Star review do aur 10% discount pao'

Yeh sabse aam galti hai jo restaurants aur salons mein hoti hai. Google ki policy saaf kehti hai ki review ke badle koi bhi upahar, discount ya free service dena sakht mana hai. Agar kisi customer ne likh diya ki 'free chai ke liye review diya', toh Google saare reviews ura dega.

Galti 2: Counter Par Tablet Rakh Kar Review Karwana

Dukan ke Wi-Fi aur IP address ka spam trap

Billing counter par tablet ya phone rakhkar customer se login karwake review karwana galat hai. Google dekhta hai ki ek hi IP address aur ek hi device se 20 reviews aa rahe hain, jisse Google use turant spam ghoshit kar deta hai.

Galti 3: Staff Aur Rishtedaron Se Reviews Likhwana

Conflict of interest ka niyam

Dukan ke karamchariyon, doston ya parivar walo se 5-star review likhwana Google ki policy ke khilaf hai. Google ka network pehchan leta hai ki yeh log aksar ek hi location ya Wi-Fi par sath rehte hain.

Galti 4: WhatsApp Ya Agency Se Reviews Kharidna

2000 rupaye mein 100 reviews wala fraud

Fake review bechne wale automated bots ya fake IDs ka istemal karte hain. Google har thode din mein aisi fake IDs ko mass-delete karta hai. Aise profiles par 'Review Shadowban' lag jata hai jiske baad asli customer ka review bhi nahi dikhta.

Galti 5: Ek Dusre Ko Review Dena (Review Exchange)

Facebook/WhatsApp groups mein aapas mein review baantna

Agar A ne B ko review diya aur B ne A ko review diya, toh Google ka algorithm is circular pattern ko aasani se pakad leta hai aur dono profiles par penalty laga deta hai.

Galti 6: Review Bombing Attack Se Bachne Ka Tarika

Agar achanak ek din mein 30 kharab reviews aa jayein

Agar kisi vajah se aapki dukan par fake negative reviews ki baadh aa jaye, toh ghabrayein nahi. Sabhi reviews ka screenshot lein aur Google Reviews Management Tool se mass attack report karein. Google aisi activity ko pehchan kar purane reviews restore kar deta hai.

Sabse Bada Niyam: Reputation banane ka ek hi sachha tarika hai—achha kaam karein, asli customer se request karein, aur har review ka aadar ke sath jawab dein.
