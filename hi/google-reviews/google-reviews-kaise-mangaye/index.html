Customer Se Google Reviews Kaise Mangaye: Bina Hichkichahat Ke Best Tarike

Khush customer aapki madad karna chahte hain, lekin dukaan se nikalte hi bhool jaate hain. Jaaniye unhe asani se 5-star review dene ke liye kaise prerit karein.

Lagatar 5-Star Reviews Pane Ka 6-Step System

Zyadatar businesses ke paas reviews isliye kam hote hain kyuki wo sahi samay par aur sahi tarike se customer se request nahi karte. Yahan 6 mukhya samasyaon ka practical samadhan diya gaya hai.

Samasya 1: Customer Ki Aalas Aur Samay Ki Kami

Customer khush hone ke baad bhi review kyu nahi likhta?

Customer ko Google Maps kholna, dukaan dhoondhna, aur review likhna ek bada kaam lagta hai. Agar isme 30 second se zyada samay lagega toh woh chhod dega. Aapko customer ko direct link dena hoga jisse ek click mein review window khul jaye.

Samasya 2: Direct 1-Click Review Link Banana

Apna Google Review link kaise nikalein?

Apne Google Business Profile par jayein, 'Ask for reviews' button par click karein aur apna short link copy karein (jaise https://g.page/r/.../review). Yeh link seedhe 5-star rating ka pop-up kholta hai.

Samasya 3: WhatsApp Aur SMS Message Templates

Asardaar message jo customer ko turant review karne par prerit kare

Service poori hone ya billing ke 1-2 ghante ke andar yeh WhatsApp message bhejein:

"Namaste [Customer Name] ji, [Dukaan Ka Naam] aane ke liye aapka shukriya! Ummeed hai aapko hamari service pasand aayi hogi. Kya aap 30 second nikal kar Google par apna anubhav share karenge? Isse hamari choti team ko bohot madad milti hai: [Direct Link]. Dhanyawad!"
Samasya 4: Billing Counter Aur QR Code Standee

Counter par baithe customer se review lene ka tarika

Apne review link ka QR code banakar billing counter par ek sundar acrylic standee lagayein. Bill invoice ke niche bhi QR code print karein aur likhein: "Hamari service kaisi lagi? Scan karke Google par batayein."

Samasya 5: Staff Ko Sahi Samay Par Bolne Ki Training

Review maangne ka sabse sahi samay kab hota hai?

Jab customer khush hokar kahe, "Bhaiya maza aa gaya!" ya "Kaam bohot badhiya kiya aapne", tabhi turant kahein: "Sir/Ma'am aapki tareef hamare liye bohot keemti hai, bas 30 second nikal kar yahi baat Google par likh dijiye na please!"

Samasya 6: 'Review Gating' Ki Galti Se Bachein

Kharab reviews ko chhipana kyu mana hai?

Kuch log customer se pehle poochte hain 'Service kaisi thi?' aur agar achhi ho tabhi Google link dete hain. Is trick ko Google Review Gating kehta hai aur yeh Google ki policy ke sakht khilaf hai. Har customer ko barabar link dein.

Zaroori Baat: Review ke badle kabhi discount, cash ya free gift na dein. Google ke algorithm ko bribery ka pata chalte hi listing suspend ho sakti hai.
