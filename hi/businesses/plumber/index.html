अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल (FAQ)

Can a plumber rank on Google Maps without a physical retail shop?

Yes! Register as a 'Service-Area Business' (SAB). Google hides your home address and shows your service coverage radius (e.g. 15 km or specific postal codes).

How do plumbers get reviews if they visit client homes?

Send an instant WhatsApp message with your direct review link immediately after completing the repair: 'Namaste [Name], thanks for choosing [Business]. Please take 30 seconds to rate our plumbing work on Google: [Link]'.

What photos can a plumber upload to build trust?

Photos of completed clean bathroom fittings, neat PVC piping installations, leak detection tools, and your branded service vehicle or uniform.
