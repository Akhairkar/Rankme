अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल (FAQ)

What attributes should bakeries highlight on Google Maps?

Highlight 'Curbside pickup', 'Delivery', and dietary attributes like 'Pure Vegetarian' or 'Gluten-free' if available. Customers search specifically for these filters.

How can bakeries collect reviews on customized cake deliveries?

Include a small decorative QR tag on the cake box: 'Hope you loved your celebration cake! Scan here to share a photo in a Google review!'
