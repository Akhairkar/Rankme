अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल (FAQ)

How can auto workshops build trust regarding genuine spare parts?

Post photos of OEM/OES spare parts packaging (Bosch, Mobil, Castrol, OEM filters) and state your parts warranty clearly in your business description.

When should mechanics request Google reviews?

Right after delivering the serviced car, handing over the itemized invoice, and doing a joint test drive with the owner.
