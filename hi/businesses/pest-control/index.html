How can pest control companies address customer safety fears?

Emphasize government-approved, CIB-registered, odorless chemicals that are safe for toddlers, elderly family members, and household pets.

How do pest control businesses get calls from Google Maps?

Set up a Service Area Business (SAB), highlight your 1-year or 2-year warranty certificate, and enable the direct phone call button.
