Report a bug

Found something broken or wrong on the website? Tell us. The form below prepares a report for you to send; nothing is sent until you press send in your email app.

Last updated 30 October 2026

Describe the problem

Optional: also send my email and a short summary to the server

Before you report

  1. Check system status in case the service is known to be unavailable.
  2. Look in the help center for your problem; many questions are answered there.
  3. Try reloading the page once, and try another browser if you can.

What helps us fix it

  • What happened, in your own words, and what you expected.
  • The page address, so we can open the same page. This is filled in for you when you arrive from a page.
  • Steps to reproduce: the exact clicks and what you typed, in order.
  • Your browser and device. We fill in your browser's description; you can edit it.

A screenshot helps. Attach it in the email, with personal details hidden.

What not to send

Never include passwords, one-time codes, card numbers, Google tokens or other people's personal data. Security problems should be reported through the security page, not here.

How this form works

This page does not have a server for bug reports. When you press the button, it builds the report text and offers an email link to support@localboost.in and a copy button. You send the email yourself, and we only receive it when you do. We cannot see your report before that, and the page will never say it was received when it was not.

If the LocalBoost API is connected to the website and you also give an email and consent, the page can additionally store your email and the first 120 characters of your description in our contact list through the same endpoint the waitlist uses. That endpoint was built for waitlist sign-ups, so it cannot keep your steps, your page address or your browser details. Treat the email as the real report.

What happens next

We read every report. We cannot promise a reply time or a fix date. If we need more detail, we reply to the email you sent from. Fixes that matter to users are listed in the changelog.

Other ways to reach us

Email support@localboost.in. We have no phone number or chat. For feature questions see features; for billing questions see the billing FAQ.
