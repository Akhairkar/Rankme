# Session 10 — BUSINESS TYPE TEMPLATE

Create a reusable content/page template for business-type local SEO pages.

Use a salon as the example, but make the template reusable for:
restaurant, doctor, dentist, gym, hotel, kirana shop, mobile shop, plumber, electrician and many others.

Every business-type page must have genuinely different useful information:
- suitable profile considerations
- relevant services
- photos
- review considerations
- website/local SEO
- common mistakes
- checklist
- relevant tools
- FAQ

Do not simply replace the business name in duplicate text.
Create the template and one high-quality example only.
