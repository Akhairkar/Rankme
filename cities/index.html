How to use these city guides

Local search differs from one city to the next in language, neighbourhood names, travel time and festival calendar, but the fundamentals are the same everywhere: an accurate Business Profile, honest services and photos, steady genuine reviews and consistent details across the web. Read your city's guide for the local flavour, then work through the general guides.

If your city is not listed yet, the same approach works: write your address the way locals say it, name the markets and landmarks your customers use, and plan around your local seasons. Start with the Business Profile audit checklist, then see local keyword research for India and multi-location local SEO if you have more than one branch. No guide can promise rankings; these pages are practical starting points.

Is LocalBoost free to use?

The guides and self-serve tools on LocalBoost are free and need no signup. Paid plans add features that need a connected Google account, such as monitoring and review management. See the Pricing page for details.

Can LocalBoost guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No. No tool or agency can guarantee rankings. Local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, plus your competition. LocalBoost shows you what to improve; it does not promise positions.

What happens to my data?

The free tools run in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Data is processed on our servers only if you choose to connect your Google account. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
