Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
In plain language: You can use our guides and free tools without an account. If you sign in with Google, we store your name and email. If you also connect Google Business Profile or Search Console, we read your profile, review and search data only to show it back to you inside LocalBoost. We never sell your data, we show no ads, payments are handled by Razorpay (we never see your card number), analytics run only if you accept cookies, and you can export or delete your data and disconnect Google at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the LocalBoost website at www.localboost.in, our free tools, and the paid dashboard ("LocalBoost", "we", "us"). It explains what personal data we process, why, who else handles it, and the choices you have. LocalBoost is an independent product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google.

2. Data we process

  • Account data: when you sign in with Google we receive your name and email address. We use them to create and identify your account and to contact you about it.
  • Google Business Profile data: if you choose to connect, we read the business locations you manage, profile details, reviews and basic performance figures through the Google Business Profile APIs. We post a reply to a review only after you confirm that specific reply.
  • Search Console data: only if you separately choose to connect Search Console, we read read-only search performance data for the site you select.
  • Information you enter: business names, keywords, competitors, website addresses, notes, and the audit and rank-scan results that LocalBoost generates for you.
  • Payment data: payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive order and payment identifiers, the plan, amount, status and GST invoice details. We do not receive or store your card, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials.
  • Waitlist and contact submissions: name, email or phone number, business name, city and your message, plus a hashed network identifier used only to limit abuse.
  • Analytics (with consent only): see section 5.
  • Technical data: a secure session cookie while you are signed in, and short-lived rate-limit counters that protect the service from abuse.

The free tools on this site (generators, checklist, QR and embed tools) run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to our servers unless a tool says so on the page.

3. How we use Google user data

We use data received from Google only to provide and improve the features you asked for inside LocalBoost: showing your locations, reviews and performance, drafting replies for you to approve, and producing audits and reports for you. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it to data brokers, and we do not use it to train generalised AI models. Humans at LocalBoost do not read your Google data unless you ask for support on a specific issue, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

LocalBoost's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect Google at any time from your account page. Disconnecting revokes the stored token with Google and deletes the saved connection; you can also remove LocalBoost's access from your Google Account permissions page.

4. Payments

Paid plans are billed through Razorpay. Razorpay is an independent payment processor with its own privacy policy. We verify payment confirmations from Razorpay and keep the resulting order, payment and invoice records. Invoices are issued as GST invoices where applicable. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

5. Analytics, cookies and local storage

  • We do not run advertising, ad networks or ad-targeting cookies.
  • Our analytics are first-party: events go to LocalBoost's own server, from a fixed list of product events, and only after you accept the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics events are sent.
  • Analytics events do not store your IP address.
  • We store your banner choice in your browser's local storage so we do not ask you on every page.
  • A strictly necessary session cookie keeps you signed in. It is HttpOnly and is removed when you sign out.

6. Where data is stored and who handles it

Account and product data are stored in Cloudflare D1, and the site and API run on Cloudflare. We do not currently promise a specific physical data-residency location. Our subprocessors and service providers are:

  • Cloudflare for hosting, the API and database.
  • Razorpay for payment processing.
  • Google APIs (Sign-In, Business Profile, Search Console) when you connect them.
  • Email provider: we will list email providers here when enabled.

7. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only with the providers above to run the service, with professional advisers under confidentiality, or when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

8. Retention, deletion and export

We keep your data while your account is active. You can download everything we hold in your account as JSON from your account page (the export endpoint is /api/account/export) and delete your account from the same page. Deleting the account removes your businesses, keywords, reviews, audits and connections from our live database. Payment and tax records may be kept for as long as the law requires. Details are on the Data Retention & Deletion page.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, withdraw consent (for example by disconnecting Google or declining analytics), and raise a complaint. We handle requests in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as it applies to us; this policy is not a certification of compliance with any law.

10. Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Google refresh tokens are encrypted before they are stored, and session identifiers are stored only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; we will tell affected users when the law requires.

11. Children

LocalBoost is built for business owners and is not directed at children. Please do not use it if you are under 18 years of age. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell signed-in users.

13. Grievance contact

Grievance contact: support@localboost.in. We aim to acknowledge complaints promptly and resolve them within a reasonable time. See also our Terms of Service and Google compliance page.
