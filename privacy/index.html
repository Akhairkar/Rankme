Privacy Policy — LocalBoost

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

LocalBoost collects minimal personal data required to deliver diagnostic audits and authenticated profile management:

  • Account Information: Name, business name, business address, email, and phone number.
  • Google Business Profile Data: When connected via OAuth, we access profile locations, public review text, and customer rating statistics authorized by you. We never request access to Gmail, Google Drive, or personal Google data.
  • Self-Audit Diagnostics: Information submitted into our client-side audit tools is processed in your browser.

2. How We Protect Your Data

All sensitive credentials and OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM encryption and transmitted over TLS 1.3. Encryption keys are stored in secure Key Management Systems.

3. No Selling of Business Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party data brokers, marketing agencies, or aggregators under any circumstances.

5. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google LLC (Google AdSense), to serve ads when you visit our website:

  • DoubleClick DART Cookies: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on LocalBoost. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to LocalBoost and/or other websites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at www.aboutads.info.
  • Third-Party Ad Networks: Third-party ad servers or networks may automatically receive your IP address when ad links appear on our site. LocalBoost has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

6. EU User Consent & Cookie Preferences

In adherence to the Google EU User Consent Policy, GDPR, and India's DPDP Act 2023, visitors have complete autonomy to accept or decline advertising and analytical cookies. You can manage or revoke your consent preferences at any time using our interactive Cookie Preferences banner.

4. Your Rights Under DPDP Act 2023

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and global privacy standards, you have the right to access, rectify, or request complete deletion of your data by emailing privacy@localboost.in.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Privacy Policy — LocalBoost

Is LocalBoost free to use?

The guides and self-serve tools on LocalBoost are free and need no signup. Paid plans add features that need a connected Google account, such as monitoring and review management. See the Pricing page for details.

Can LocalBoost guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No. No tool or agency can guarantee rankings. Local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, plus your competition. LocalBoost shows you what to improve; it does not promise positions.

What happens to my data?

The free tools run in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Data is processed on our servers only if you choose to connect your Google account. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
