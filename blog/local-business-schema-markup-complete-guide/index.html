Complete LocalBusiness JSON-LD Schema Guide for 2026

9 min read • August 10, 2026 • By LocalBoost Research Team

Structured data markup is the technical bridge connecting your business website directly to Google's Knowledge Graph. Implementing clean JSON-LD schema confirms your physical authenticity.

1. The Fundamental LocalBusiness Schema Block

Always declare exact latitude and longitude coordinates matching your Google Maps pin, alongside exact address components (streetAddress, addressLocality, postalCode, addressCountry).

2. Detailed Opening Hours Specification

Declare your precise opening and closing times for each day of the week using openingHoursSpecification to prevent Google from displaying conflicting hours.

3. Linking Your Official Place ID & Social Citations

Use the hasMap and sameAs properties to cross-reference your official Google Maps URL, Justdial listing, and social profiles.

What to include and how to test it

A basic LocalBusiness (or a more specific type such as Dentist, Restaurant or HomeAndConstructionBusiness) block should include the business name, URL, logo or image, telephone, address, geo coordinates, opening hours and, if relevant, price range and the areas served. Add sameAs links to your real profiles on Google Maps and social media. Use only information that is visible on the page and matches your Business Profile exactly.

Place the JSON-LD in the head or body of the relevant page, usually the home page or the location page, and test it with Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator. Fix errors and warnings that apply. Avoid marking up reviews you wrote yourself or ratings that are not shown to visitors, since this can be treated as misleading. Schema does not guarantee rich results or better rankings; it helps search engines read your page accurately.

Example fields: name, address, telephone, openingHoursSpecification, geo, areaServed, url, image and sameAs. Keep a copy of the same details in your master sheet so every location page stays consistent. For several branches, add one block per location page.
