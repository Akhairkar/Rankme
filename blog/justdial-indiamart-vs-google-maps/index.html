Google Business Profile vs. Justdial & IndiaMART: The Real ROI Comparison

11 min read • July 30, 2026 • By LocalBoost Research Team

Indian small business owners spend lakhs annually on directory packages. Comparing customer intent, inquiry quality, and long-term ownership reveals where your marketing focus belongs.

1. Shared Leads vs. Direct Exclusive Inquiries

Directory portals frequently sell a single customer lead to 4 to 8 competing vendors simultaneously, triggering an aggressive price race to the bottom. In contrast, a customer calling directly from Google Maps calls exclusively your business.

2. Recurring Directory Fees vs. Permanent Digital Equity

When you stop paying directory subscription fees, your listing visibility drops to zero immediately. In contrast, building 150 verified reviews and high local 3-pack rank on Google Maps creates permanent digital equity that continues generating free leads for years.

3. High Commercial Intent on Google Maps

Customers searching 'near me' on Google Maps have immediate buying intent and are located within your physical radius. Many local searches on a phone lead to a call or a visit soon after, which is why accurate hours, a working phone number and a correct pin matter.

How to use them together

Directories and Google are not enemies. Justdial and IndiaMART can be useful for B2B and for some service categories where buyers compare many vendors. The key is ownership and cost per enquiry: track where each call or WhatsApp message came from by asking "how did you find us?" and noting it in a sheet for three months. Then compare what each channel cost you with the customers it brought.

Make sure your name, address and phone are identical on every platform, because inconsistent listings confuse customers. See local citations and NAP consistency. Be cautious with any package promising a fixed number of leads or first position, and read the contract for renewal and cancellation terms. Whatever you pay for, keep your Google Business Profile complete, because it is free and you control it.
