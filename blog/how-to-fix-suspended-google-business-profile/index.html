How to Fix a Suspended Google Business Profile: Step-by-Step Reinstatement

August 15, 2026 • 11 min read • By LocalBoost Research

Having your Google Business Profile suspended can eliminate phone inquiries and foot traffic overnight. Understanding Google's reinstatement appeal workflow is essential to getting back on Google Maps without losing customer reviews.

1. Distinguishing Soft Suspensions vs. Hard Suspensions

A Soft Suspension occurs when your listing remains visible to the public on Google Maps, but you lose managerial access in your dashboard. This is usually triggered by unverified edits to your business name, address, or category. A Hard Suspension completely removes your listing and customer reviews from Google Maps search results, typically triggered by perceived policy violations or suspected deceptive location claims.

2. Critical Verification Documents Needed for Appeal

Before submitting Google's official reinstatement appeal form, collect clear digital scans of the following official documents:

  • Official Electricity or Water Utility Bill matching the exact business name and address.
  • GST Registration Certificate (GSTIN) or Shop & Establishment Act License (Gumasta).
  • Clear exterior storefront photo showing fixed, permanent signage matching your registered name.
  • Rental lease agreement or municipal property tax receipt.

3. Preparing for Google Video Verification

Most 2026 reinstatements require a continuous, unedited 60-second video recording via your smartphone. The video must show: the exterior street sign, permanent entrance branding, opening the locks or doors, and interior workspace equipment or billing POS machines.

4. Avoiding the Single Biggest Reinstatement Mistake

Never submit multiple duplicate appeal tickets while your first ticket is pending. Submitting multiple appeals creates conflicting support queues, resetting your review timeline back to zero.

Before you appeal

Read the notice carefully and compare your profile with Google's current guidelines. Common causes include a business name with extra keywords, an address that is a virtual office or not staffed during stated hours, a category that does not fit, or content that breaks policy. Fix those issues first, then use the reinstatement option in your profile and be ready with supporting proof such as a licence, GST registration or shop-act licence, utility bill, storefront photos showing your signboard and photos of the inside.

Be truthful and consistent, and do not submit the same appeal repeatedly with different details. Decisions take time and can be refused if the business does not meet the guidelines. Do not create a new profile for the same location, which can make matters worse. After reinstatement, run the profile audit checklist to avoid a repeat.
