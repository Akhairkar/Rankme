Connect your Google Business Profile

Permissions, what is read, choosing a location, disconnecting.

Last updated 30 October 2026

AvailabilityParts of this guide describe the signed-in product. They become available when the production API is online. That API is not connected to this website yet, so today only the free tools work without an account. The Business Profile API also needs Google approval for LocalBoost, which is still pending, so connecting will be switched on only after that.

What connecting does

Connecting lets LocalBoost read details of the Business Profile locations your Google account manages, so that audits use real data rather than what you type, and so that reviews and performance numbers can appear in your dashboard. You can use LocalBoost without connecting; those checks are then marked as provided by you.

What you are asked to allow

Sign-in asks Google for your basic identity (name and email). Connecting a profile asks for the Business Profile permission, which Google names business.manage. Google does not offer a read-only version of that permission, so the screen will say it lets the app manage your listing. In LocalBoost, reading is the default. We only write to your profile for actions you start and confirm yourself, such as posting a review reply. Search Console is a separate request, made only if you choose to connect it, and it is read-only (webmasters.readonly). We ask for each permission when you need the feature, not all at once.

How the connection works

  1. Choose Connect Google in your dashboard. You are sent to Google's own consent page. LocalBoost never sees your Google password.
  2. Approve the permissions. Google sends you back to LocalBoost with a one-time code.
  3. LocalBoost exchanges the code for tokens on the server. The long-lived refresh token is encrypted before it is stored, and it is never shown in the page or in any API response.
  4. LocalBoost lists the accounts and locations you can access. You select the location that belongs to this business. Only that location is synced.
  5. A sync reads the profile fields, reviews and, where you allow it, performance numbers. The time of the last sync is shown.

Who can connect

The Google account you use must be an owner or manager of the Business Profile. If the profile belongs to a client or colleague, ask them to add your Google account as a manager inside Google, or sign in with an account that already has that role. Never ask anyone for their password.

What is stored and for how long

Profile details and review text read from Google are kept as a cache, with a limited retention period, and refreshed while you stay connected. Counts and averages that contain no Google content are kept. Details are in privacy FAQ and the data retention page. Retention periods will be re-checked against Google's policies before launch.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect at any time from your account page. LocalBoost then revokes the refresh token at Google, deletes the stored connection, and removes cached Google review content and Search Console rows on the next cleanup run. Your business records stay unless you delete them. You can also remove LocalBoost's access directly from your Google Account's third-party access settings; the next sync will then report that the connection expired.

What can go wrong

  • "This app isn't verified" or access blocked. Google shows this until the app completes verification. Do not proceed if you see a warning you do not expect; contact us.
  • No locations found. The signed-in account may not manage any profile, or the profile may not be verified.
  • Sync says connection expired. You revoked access or the token was invalidated. Connect again.

The troubleshooting guide covers each case in detail.

What we do not do

We do not edit your hours, categories, photos or description. We do not post anything to Google without your explicit confirmation. We are not affiliated with Google and do not claim to be.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Google say LocalBoost can manage my listing?
Google offers a single Business Profile permission covering read and write. LocalBoost reads by default and writes only for actions you confirm.
Can I connect several locations?
Each business record is linked to one selected location. The number of businesses and locations depends on your plan.
Does disconnecting delete my business?
No. It revokes access and clears cached Google data. Your business records remain until you delete them.
