Team and permissions

Roles, invitations, seats, and what is available.

Last updated 30 October 2026

AvailabilityTeam workspaces exist in the backend and its tests. The screens to manage a team are being finished, so you cannot use them yet. Parts of this guide describe the signed-in product. They become available when the production API is online. That API is not connected to this website yet, so today only the free tools work without an account.

How access works

Every person signs in with their own Google account. Passwords are not shared or stored. Each user has a personal workspace; a team workspace holds businesses that several people work on.

Roles

RoleCan do
------
OwnerEverything, including billing, renaming the workspace and transferring ownership
AdminManage the team and attach businesses, plus everything a manager can do. Cannot change billing, the owner or other admins
ManagerRun audits and scans, generate reports, reply to reviews after confirmation
AnalystRead everything in the workspace, including the member list. No changes
ClientRead-only access to the businesses assigned to them, and nothing else. Sees only themselves in the member list

A workspace always keeps at least one owner.

Inviting someone

Owners and admins invite by email and choose a role. For a client, you also choose which businesses they can see. LocalBoost creates a one-time invitation link that is shown to you once, and sent by email only when email sending is configured. The link works for seven days. The invited person must sign in with the same Google account email the invitation was sent to; otherwise the invite is refused and stays unused. You can revoke an invitation any time before it is accepted.

Seats

The number of people in a workspace, including pending invitations, is limited by the owner's plan: one on Free (the owner only), three on Pro and twenty-five on Agency, at the planned limits. Over the limit, an invitation is refused with a message saying an upgrade is needed.

Changing and removing members

Owners and admins can change a role, suspend a member (which blocks all access until reactivated) or remove a member. Removing a member and transferring ownership need you to have signed in within the last fifteen minutes; if not, you are asked to sign in again. The previous owner becomes an admin after a transfer. A member can leave a team workspace; the personal workspace cannot be left.

Tracking what happened

Owners and admins can read an audit log of team actions such as invitations, role changes, removals and ownership transfers. It never contains tokens, and IP addresses are stored only as hashes and not shown.

Signing out everywhere

You can see your active sessions (creation and expiry times only) and revoke all of them, including the current one. Do this if you lose a device.

Google Business Profile access is separate

LocalBoost roles control who can see data inside LocalBoost. They do not give anyone access to your Google profile. Access to the profile itself is managed in Google by adding managers to the listing. If a colleague connects Google, they must be a manager there. See connect your Google Business Profile.

What is not available

Bulk import, a separate client portal beyond the client role, and custom roles are not available. Features marked as backend only above will be announced in the changelog when their screens are live.

Good practice for agencies

Give each colleague their own sign-in rather than sharing one account, so the audit log shows who did what. Use the analyst role for people who only need to read, and the client role for customers. Review the member list every few months and remove people who have left. When a client ends the relationship, remove their Google manager access inside Google as well as their access in LocalBoost, because the two are separate.

Frequently asked questions

Can a client see my other clients?
No. A client sees only the businesses assigned to them and only themselves in the member list.
Can I add a teammate on the free plan?
Free includes one seat, which is the owner. More seats need a paid plan.
Is two-factor sign-in available?
Sign-in uses your Google account, so use Google security features such as 2-Step Verification there.
