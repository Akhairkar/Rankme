Billing and GST FAQ

Payments, GST invoices, cancelling and refunds.

Last updated 30 October 2026

AvailabilityPaid plans are launching and no payment is being taken on this website today. This FAQ describes how billing is designed to work. The refund terms are drafts and are being confirmed.

Can I pay today?

No. Pro and Agency are launching, and checkout will be switched on only after our payment account is approved. If you see any page asking for payment before we announce launch, do not pay; tell us at support@localboost.in. Join the waitlist to be told when plans open.

Who processes payments?

Razorpay. Card, UPI, netbanking and wallet details are entered on Razorpay's checkout, which loads only when you start a payment. LocalBoost does not receive or store your card number. We store the order and payment identifiers, the amount, the plan and the status that Razorpay reports.

How do I know a payment is genuine?

After checkout, the server verifies the payment signature from Razorpay before activating a plan. Payment status changes also arrive through signed server-to-server notifications, which are checked and processed only once. A plan is never activated just because a page says so.

What do prices include?

Prices on the pricing page are shown excluding GST. GST at 18 percent, split as 9 percent CGST and 9 percent SGST, is added at checkout and shown on your invoice. The invoice records the taxable amount, each tax line and the total.

Monthly and annual plans

Monthly plans renew every month, and annual plans are paid once for twelve months at a lower monthly equivalent. A plan runs for its paid period; when the period ends without renewal the plan returns to Free. Your data is kept, but paid features switch off.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from your billing page. Cancelling stops renewal and the plan stays active until the end of the paid period. Details of what is refundable are in the refund policy.

Refunds

We always refund payments that failed after money was debited, duplicate payments, a wrong amount compared with what was shown at checkout and charges after a confirmed cancellation. Other cases are described in the refund policy, whose terms are still to be confirmed. To ask, email support@localboost.in with the payment or order identifier from your invoice. We reply with a decision, normally within five working days.

Invoices

Every successful payment produces a numbered GST invoice that you can download from the billing page. If you need your business name or GSTIN on it, tell us before paying, as invoices cannot be edited afterwards; corrections are made with a credit note.

What happens to invoices if I delete my account?

This is under review. Tax rules may require us to retain invoice records, while account deletion currently removes your data. The final rule will be stated on the data retention page before paid plans launch.

Plan limits

Each plan has limits for businesses, locations, keywords, audits per month, rank scans per month, competitors and crawl pages. Exceeding a limit stops that action and shows which plan is needed; nothing is charged automatically for going over. Current limits are on the pricing page.

Who do I contact?

support@localboost.in. We do not have a phone number.

Common situations

  • I paid but my plan is not active. The plan is activated only after the server verifies the payment, and a signed notification from Razorpay can arrive a little after you return to the page. Refresh after a minute. If it is still inactive, email support@localboost.in with the payment or order identifier; do not pay again.
  • My bank debited me but the payment failed. This is one of the cases we always refund. The money normally returns to your account through the bank within the time Razorpay and your bank take, which is outside our control.
  • I need an invoice in my company name. Tell us the legal name and GSTIN before paying. If you already paid, ask us to issue a credit note and a corrected invoice.
  • I want to switch from monthly to annual. Plan changes are handled at the end of the current period so you are not charged twice for the same time.

Frequently asked questions

Is GST included in the price?
No. Prices are shown excluding GST, which is added at checkout and shown on the invoice.
Do you store my card details?
No. Razorpay handles card and UPI details. We store identifiers and the payment status only.
Can I get a refund?
Failed, duplicate, wrong-amount and post-cancellation charges are always refunded. See the refund policy for the rest.
