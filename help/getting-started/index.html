Getting started with LocalBoost

What to do first, with and without an account.

Last updated 30 October 2026

AvailabilityParts of this guide describe the signed-in product. They become available when the production API is online. That API is not connected to this website yet, so today only the free tools work without an account.

Step 1: use the free tools, no account needed

Everything in the free tier runs in your browser. Open the free business audit, answer the questions about your Google Business Profile and website, and you receive a score with a prioritised fix list. Nothing needs to be installed, no password is created and no card is requested. Other free tools are the local SEO checklist, the description generator, the review reply generator and the rank grid simulator, which is a teaching aid and does not show real rankings.

Your answers are used to compute the result in the page. Whether anything is stored depends on you: if you choose to join the waitlist from the result page, the details you type into that form are sent. Read privacy FAQ for the exact behaviour.

Step 2: understand the result

The audit score is a summary of how complete and consistent your listed information is. It is not a prediction of where you will appear in Google. Work through the list in order: high severity items first, then quick fixes that take about fifteen minutes. Understanding your audit score explains every section.

Step 3: decide whether you need the paid product

The paid plans, Pro and Agency, add saved history, live data from your Google Business Profile, rank scans, review monitoring, competitor tracking and reports. They are launching. They depend on the production API, on Google approving access to Business Profile data and on a licensed rank-data provider. You can see current plans and limits on the pricing page, and you can join the waitlist to be told when they open. Joining does not commit you to anything.

Signing in once the product is live

LocalBoost uses Google sign-in only. There is no LocalBoost password to remember or leak. When you sign in, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and a session cookie keeps you signed in. The cookie is not readable by scripts on the page and expires on a schedule enforced by the server.

After signing in, the first-run flow is:

  1. Create a business record with its name, city and category, and your website address.
  2. Optionally connect your Google Business Profile. This is a separate permission step; see connect your Google Business Profile.
  3. Run your first audit. See the website audit guide.
  4. Add the keywords you care about, then run a rank scan if your plan includes it. See the rank tracking guide.
  5. Review the 30-day plan and mark tasks as done when you finish them.

What to expect in the first week

Most owners gain more from fixing five basics than from any tool: choose the most specific primary category, set accurate hours, add a phone number that matches your website, write a clear description, and reply to every review. These are the high-weight items in the audit model. Do them first and re-run the audit to see the change.

Limits to keep in mind

  • Free accounts have a small monthly audit allowance and no rank scans.
  • Data shown as "provided by you" is not verified against Google.
  • If something is unavailable, the product says so rather than showing a zero. For example, a rank position that was not found is empty, not 0.
  • We never guarantee rankings, calls or revenue.

Getting help

If you get stuck, read the relevant guide in this help center, use the search on the help home, or email support@localboost.in with the page address and what you expected. For a problem with the site itself, use the bug report form.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use LocalBoost?
No. The free tools work without one. A Google sign-in is needed only for the paid product once it opens.
Is there a password?
No. Sign-in is through Google, so we never store a password.
Can I use LocalBoost for more than one business?
Yes, with limits set by your plan. Free covers one business. See the pricing page for current limits.
