Troubleshooting the Google connection

Blocked sign-in, no locations, expired connection, sync errors.

Last updated 30 October 2026

AvailabilityParts of this guide describe the signed-in product. They become available when the production API is online. That API is not connected to this website yet, so today only the free tools work without an account. Google must also approve access for LocalBoost first; until then connecting is not switched on.

First, check the basics

  1. You are signed in to the Google account that manages the Business Profile, not a different personal account.
  2. The profile is verified in Google. Unverified profiles may not be returned by the API.
  3. Your browser allows pop-ups and third-party sign-in windows from accounts.google.com.
  4. You are using a recent browser with cookies for localboost.in allowed. The session cookie is needed after you return from Google.

"Access blocked" or "This app isn't verified"

Google shows this warning for apps that have not completed its verification. If you see it, close the window. We will not ask you to click through a warning. Verification is part of what is pending before launch. If you are sure the page is the real LocalBoost sign-in and you see this message after launch, email us.

No locations were found

  • The Google account you used does not manage any location. Ask the owner to add you as a manager, then reconnect.
  • The listing is a service-area business without a storefront; it should still appear, but check inside Google that it is listed under your account.
  • Locations are grouped under accounts, including organisation or location groups. We list all accounts you can access; choose the right one.
  • A newly added permission can take a few minutes to appear.

The connection says expired

Your refresh token stopped working. Common causes are removing LocalBoost in your Google Account's third-party access page, changing your Google password with some security settings, or the token being unused for a long period. Connect again; reviews and data you already synced stay available until their cache period ends.

Review sync returns nothing

Check that a location is selected, the last sync time, and whether the profile has reviews. Reviews are fetched in batches and Google may limit how often. Try again later. If the state says awaiting a Google location, no location is linked yet.

Replies fail to post

Replies are posted only after you confirm. If posting fails, the review may have been deleted or the account may no longer have manager access. Copy your reply text first so it is not lost.

Search Console will not connect

Search Console is a separate permission requested on its own. The Google account must have access to the property, and the property must be the right type: a domain property or a URL prefix property that matches your website. Pick the property from the list that LocalBoost shows after you allow access.

Performance numbers are missing

Performance data comes from Google with delays and may not exist for every period or location. Missing periods are shown as unavailable, not as zero.

If a problem remains

Disconnect and reconnect, then try one more sync. If it still fails, email support@localboost.in with the time of the attempt, the Google account email you used, the error text you saw and a screenshot with personal details hidden. Do not send passwords or codes. You can also use the bug report form.

Quick checklist before you email us

  • Which Google account did you sign in with, and is it a manager of the profile in Google?
  • Do you see the profile when you open business.google.com with that account?
  • What exact message appeared, and at which step: sign-in, location list, sync or reply?
  • Did it work earlier and stop, or has it never worked?

Having these four answers lets us help you in one reply instead of five.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I see an unverified app warning?
Google shows this until an app finishes its verification. We will not ask you to bypass such a warning.
Can I connect a profile I do not own?
Only if your Google account has been given manager access by the owner, inside Google.
Does reconnecting delete my data?
No. Your businesses and audits remain. Only Google caches follow their own retention rules.
