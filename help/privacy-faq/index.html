Privacy FAQ

What we collect, analytics consent, Google data, your rights.

Last updated 30 October 2026

AvailabilityThis FAQ summarises behaviour in the code. The legal wording is in the Privacy Policy, which is a draft awaiting legal review.

What do you collect when I just browse?

The website itself is static pages. Your browser requests fonts from Google Fonts. If you accept the cookie banner, a first-party analytics script sends a few events to our own server. If you decline, it sends nothing. See the cookie policy for the exact list.

What does analytics record?

Only if you accept: the page path without the query string, language, and for some events the tool or plan involved, for a fixed list of events such as page view or tool used. No user identifier, IP address, user agent or free text is stored. To count unique visitors without following people, the server uses a daily hash of IP address, browser and date mixed with a secret; the hash changes every day. Raw events are kept for 90 days, then reduced to daily totals with no identifiers.

What do the free tools do with what I type?

They run in your browser. Some keep your answers in your browser's storage for convenience, for example so a long form is not lost if you reload. Nothing is sent to us unless you press a button that says it sends, such as joining the waitlist.

What happens when I join the waitlist or contact you?

We receive the fields you enter: email or mobile number, optional business name and city, your consent and the page you joined from. You must tick a consent box. We use it to tell you about launch. Email support@localboost.in from that address to have it removed.

What do you collect when I sign in?

Name, email and profile picture from Google, plus a session identifier. The session cookie is HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite, and only a hash of it is stored on the server. Your Google refresh token, if you connect a profile, is encrypted before storage.

How is Google data used?

Only to show you your own information inside LocalBoost: audits, reviews, performance and Search Console summaries. We do not sell it, use it for advertising or share it with other customers. Cached Google review text and similar content expires on a schedule and is deleted when you disconnect Google. See data export and deletion.

Do you show ads or use advertising cookies?

No. There are no advertising scripts or tracking pixels.

Do you use AI on my data?

By default, no external AI service is used. If a language model is configured in future, the assistant sends a limited set of your stored business facts, with emails and phone numbers removed, and the privacy policy will list the provider before it is switched on.

Who else sees my data?

Service providers needed to run the product: our hosting and database provider, Razorpay when you pay, Google for sign-in and the data you connect, and an email provider once email sending is enabled. We do not sell personal data.

What are my rights?

You can export your data as a file, disconnect Google, delete your account and ask us to correct or remove waitlist entries. Write to support@localboost.in for anything the product does not offer yet.

Is LocalBoost affiliated with Google?

No. We are an independent product.

How you can check this yourself

You do not have to take our word for it. Open your browser's developer tools on any page, go to the Network tab and decline the cookie banner. You should see no requests to our analytics endpoint. Accept the banner and you should see small requests that contain only a path, a language and an event name. The code that does this is the file assets/analytics.js, and it loads only after you accept. If you find behaviour that does not match this FAQ, please tell us through the bug report form.

Frequently asked questions

Do you track me without consent?
No analytics events are sent unless you accept the cookie banner.
Do you sell my data?
No. We do not sell personal data and we run no advertising.
Where can I read the legal policy?
At the Privacy Policy page, which is currently a draft awaiting legal review.
