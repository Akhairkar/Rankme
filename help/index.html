    Trust, policies and status

      How this help center is organised

      The guides follow the order most people need them. Getting started explains what works today without an account. Connect your Google Business Profile and Troubleshooting the Google connection cover the most common source of questions. The audit and rank tracking guides explain how to read results, including what a missing or empty value means. Billing and GST, Privacy and Data export and deletion describe money and data in plain language.

      Status of the product, in one paragraph

      The free tools work now. The signed-in product, with saved audits, Google data, rank scans, reports and teams, is built in the backend and tested with mocked services, but it has not been connected to this website or run against live Google or payment accounts. Guides that describe it say so at the top. We would rather be clear than have you rely on something that is not switched on.

      Need a person?

      Email support@localboost.in. Include the page address and what you expected. We have no phone line. For anything that looks like a fault in the website, use the bug report form.
