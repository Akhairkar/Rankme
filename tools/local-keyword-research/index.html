How the ideas are generated (rule set)
  • Core: "service city", "service in city", plus best / top / affordable / cheap / trusted, and 24x7 only if you tick it.
  • Near me: "service near me", "best service near me", "service nearby", "service near me open now".
  • Locality: "service in locality", "service near locality", "best service in locality city" for every locality you list.
  • Questions: "which is the best service in city", "where to find ...", "how to choose a ...", "how much does ... cost in city".
  • Price and comparison: price, cost, charges, rates, cheap, reviews, and "service A vs service B" when you list more than one service.
  • Hinglish and Hindi: "city mein service", "service kaise", "service kitne ka hota hai", and Devanagari versions built around the words you type. Type Devanagari service names to get fully Devanagari ideas.
  • Duplicates are removed (case-insensitive). At most 600 ideas are shown.
How intent is labelled (simple rules, first match wins)
  • Navigational: contains contact number, address, timings, website, or is your business name.
  • Informational: starts with how / what / which / where / why, or contains kaise / kya / kaun / kitne, "vs", "guide", "tips".
  • Commercial: contains best, top, affordable, cheap, reviews, price, cost, rates, charges, compare.
  • Transactional: contains near me, nearby, book, appointment, 24x7, open now, call, order.
  • Anything else (a plain "service in place") is treated as transactional, because it is a local buying search.

The labels are rule-based guesses for planning, not data from Google.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Local Keyword Ideas Generator

Does this show search volume or difficulty?

No. The ideas are generated from patterns, so there are no volume, difficulty or ranking numbers. Check demand in Google Keyword Planner or Search Console before relying on any idea.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Everything is generated in your browser. The optional saved lists are stored only in your own browser storage and can be deleted at any time.

How is intent decided?

With simple documented rules based on words in the phrase, such as "near me", "best", "price" or "how". They are planning labels, not Google data, and some phrases will not fit perfectly.

Can I use the ideas for my Google Business Profile?

Use them to plan website pages, service descriptions and FAQs. Do not stuff keywords into your business name; Google requires the name to match your real-world name.

Is the tool free?

Yes. It is free, needs no signup and costs INR 0.
