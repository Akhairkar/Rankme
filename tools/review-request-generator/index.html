Review Request Message Generator

Create WhatsApp, SMS and email messages that ask customers for an honest Google review. Edit them, then send them yourself, one customer at a time.

Google review rules to follow
  • No incentives: do not offer discounts, gifts, cash or entry to a draw in return for a review.
  • No gating: ask every customer, not only the ones who seem happy, and do not steer unhappy customers away from reviewing.
  • No fake reviews: never write reviews for customers or post reviews from your own devices or staff.
  • One request per customer: send a single polite request and do not keep reminding.
  • Ask for honest feedback. Do not tell people what to write or what rating to give.
Use the official link from your Google Business Profile ("Ask for reviews"), or a writereview link built with your Place ID. Accepted: g.page, search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=, maps.app.goo.gl.
Only used to prefill the WhatsApp and SMS buttons on your device. It is not stored or sent to LocalBoost.

Is LocalBoost free to use?

The guides and self-serve tools on LocalBoost are free and need no signup. Paid plans add features that need a connected Google account, such as monitoring and review management. See the Pricing page for details.

Can LocalBoost guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

No. No tool or agency can guarantee rankings. Local results depend on relevance, distance and prominence, plus your competition. LocalBoost shows you what to improve; it does not promise positions.

What happens to my data?

The free tools run in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. Data is processed on our servers only if you choose to connect your Google account. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
