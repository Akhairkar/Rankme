Google Review Policy Dispute & Appeal Drafter

Draft a factual message to ask Google to review a review that you believe breaks its policy. It uses only what you enter and does not submit anything.

Be accurate and calm. Do not threaten legal action, do not claim facts you have not checked, and do not publicly name or accuse the reviewer. Removal is never guaranteed, and this is not legal advice.
The usual order of steps
  1. Reply politely only if the review is a genuine complaint; otherwise do not reply.
  2. Flag the review from your Business Profile (in the reviews list, use the menu on the review and choose Report review).
  3. Check the status in Google's review-removal tools and use the appeal option in Business Profile Help if it is not removed.
  4. Use this draft as the wording for the explanation or appeal. Google makes the final decision.
