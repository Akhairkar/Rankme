Website SEO Checker for Local Businesses

Check one public page of your website for on-page SEO and local-business signals. Every finding comes with evidence and a suggested fix.

What this checks: public, on-page signals of one page (title, description, headings, links, images, structured data, mobile viewport, indexability, local signals such as phone, address and map). It does not measure rankings, traffic or reviews, and no overall score is shown unless the checker returns one.
Live checker connects when the production API is online. The live checker is not switched on yet, so this page cannot fetch websites right now. Nothing has been sent anywhere. In the meantime you can run the single-page audit that works entirely in your browser: Open the Free Business & Website Audit

Privacy: when you click the button, the address you enter is sent to the LocalBoost API, and our server fetches that public page. Nothing else from this page is sent. The free checker allows 10 checks per hour from one network and reads one page.

What the checker looks at
  • Page status, redirects, robots.txt rules and meta robots / noindex.
  • Title and meta description presence and length, H1 count, heading order, canonical and hreflang.
  • Images without alt text, mobile viewport tag, html lang attribute, Open Graph tags.
  • JSON-LD structured data, including LocalBusiness type and key properties.
  • Local signals: tel: link or phone number, street address, contact link and embedded map.
  • Internal link count and a sample of internal links for errors.

Limits: one page per check, public pages only, pages larger than 1.5 MB are not analysed, and each request waits at most 8 seconds. Findings are automated and may include false positives, so check them before you change anything.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about Website SEO Checker

What does the Website SEO Checker check?

It reads one public page and reports on-page SEO and local-business signals: title, description, headings, links, images, structured data, mobile viewport, indexability and local signals such as phone number, address and map.

Is the address I enter sent to a server?

Yes. This tool is different from our browser-only tools: when you press Check website, the address is sent to the LocalBoost API, which fetches the public page. Nothing else is sent. If the live checker is not online yet, nothing is sent at all.

Does it give a ranking score?

No. It lists issues with evidence and fixes. It does not predict rankings and shows no overall score unless the checker returns one.

Why was my website blocked or unreachable?

Only public web pages on standard ports can be checked. Localhost, private addresses, raw IPs, links with a password and non-web schemes are blocked. A site can also be unreachable if it is offline, too slow (over 8 seconds), redirects in a loop, or its robots.txt disallows our checker.

Is it free?

Yes. The single-page checker is free and costs INR 0, limited to 10 checks per hour from one network.
