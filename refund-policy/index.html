Billing & Refunds

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026
In plain language: You can cancel any time and keep your plan until the period you paid for ends. Monthly payments are not refunded once the month has started. If you buy an annual plan for the first time and have not used it beyond its limits, you can ask for a refund within 7 days. Failed or duplicate payments are always refunded. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to paid LocalBoost plans (Pro and Agency, billed monthly or annually) that you purchase through our checkout, where payment is processed by Razorpay. It sits alongside our Terms of Service. Free tools, guides and the free plan cost nothing and have nothing to refund. Current prices and plan limits are on the Pricing page.

2. Cancelling a plan

You can cancel at any time from your account page; you do not need to give a reason or contact us. Cancelling stops future renewals. Your paid features stay active until the end of the period you have already paid for (the end of the month for a monthly plan, or the end of the year for an annual plan), after which the account moves to the free plan. We do not charge a cancellation fee. Cancelling does not delete your data; if you also want it deleted, see Data Retention & Deletion.

3. Monthly plans

Monthly plan payments are non-refundable once the billing month has begun, because access starts immediately. If you cancel part-way through the month, you keep access until the month ends and are not charged again. The exceptions in section 5 (failed, duplicate or wrongly charged payments) still apply.

4. Annual plans

If you buy an annual plan for the first time, you may ask for a full refund within 7 days of the payment, provided your use has stayed within the free plan's limits (for example, you have not used the paid plan's additional businesses, keywords or scans beyond what the free plan allows). This is meant to let you try the paid features fairly while protecting the cost of usage-based services such as data providers. After 7 days, or on renewal of an annual plan, annual payments are non-refundable, but you can cancel to stop the next renewal. We will send a reminder before an annual renewal where our systems can do so.

5. Payments we always refund

  • Failed payments where money was debited but the plan was not activated.
  • Duplicate payments for the same plan and period.
  • Wrong amount charged compared with the price shown at checkout.
  • Charges after a confirmed cancellation that should not have been taken.

Often a failed payment is reversed automatically by the bank or Razorpay without any action from you. If it has not been reversed within the timelines below, write to us with the payment ID.

6. If a feature does not work

If a paid feature you rely on is unavailable because of a problem on our side, tell us at support@localboost.in. We will try to fix it first. If we cannot, we may extend your plan or refund the affected period at our reasonable discretion. We do not refund because a ranking, review count or business result did not change, because we do not promise those outcomes; see the Disclaimer.

7. How to ask for a refund

  1. Email support@localboost.in from the address on your account.
  2. Include the Razorpay payment ID or order ID (it is on your payment confirmation and invoice), the plan, and the reason.
  3. We will reply with a decision, usually within 5 working days.

8. Timelines and how the money returns

Approved refunds are issued through Razorpay to the original payment method (card, UPI, net banking or wallet). After we initiate a refund, banks and card networks generally take about 5 to 10 working days to show it, and the time can differ between banks. We cannot speed up a bank's processing, but we will share the refund reference so you can follow it up.

9. GST invoices and credit notes

We issue GST invoices for paid plans where applicable. If you ask for a GSTIN to be added, please send it before or at the time of purchase, since correcting an invoice later may need a credit note and a fresh invoice. When a payment is refunded, we will adjust the invoice records as required by law.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the new version applies to purchases made after the change. Changes will be marked by the date at the top of this page. Grievance contact: support@localboost.in.
