Review management: see, answer and learn from Google reviews

Replying to reviews is one of the few local-search tasks entirely in your control. LocalBoost helps you see every review, draft thoughtful replies and keep a record, without posting anything you have not approved.

AvailabilityFree reply, request and dispute generators work today without signing in. Fetching your real reviews and posting replies needs Google sign-in and approval for the Business Profile API, which is still pending. The paid review workflow is launching.

The problem it solves

Most owners know they should reply to reviews but lose track of which ones are unanswered, forget what they said last time and are unsure how to answer a harsh review calmly. Review management puts the reviews, the reply status and the drafting help in one place.

What works today and what is waiting

Works today, free, no sign-in: the review reply generator, the review request generator and the review dispute generator. They run in your browser. You paste a review or describe a situation and get editable draft text.

Waiting on Google approval and the production API: fetching your actual reviews, showing which are unanswered and publishing replies from LocalBoost. The code for these steps exists in the backend and has been tested against mocked responses only. It has never run against live Google data, so we describe it as designed behaviour, not as a live service.

How replies are posted safely

A reply is sent to Google only when the request carries an explicit confirmation. Without it the server refuses with a "confirmation required" error. There is no auto-reply that posts on its own; drafts are suggestions, and you decide the final text. We also separate permissions: sign-in starts with read-only access where possible, and a write permission is asked for only when you choose to post.

Writing replies that help

A good reply thanks the person by name, references one specific detail, stays calm if the review is negative, invites offline resolution with a way to reach you and never reveals private details. Our guide to replying to Google reviews goes through this step by step, with examples for clinics, salons, restaurants and shops.

What we do not do

We do not sell reviews, write fake reviews, offer incentives for reviews or help you hide negative ones. Google's policies forbid these. We cannot remove reviews; we explain how to flag ones that break the rules in the fake review guide, and Google decides the outcome.

Limitations

  • Review data arrives only after you connect Google and the account has the right access to the location.
  • Google may limit how often reviews can be fetched; the app shows the time of the last sync.
  • Sentiment labels are only a sorting aid.
  • Replies in languages other than English or Hindi depend on your own edits.

Plan availability

Free accounts keep using the generators. Review monitoring and reply posting belong to Pro and Agency, shown on the pricing page. Join the waitlist below for launch news.

Frequently asked questions

Will LocalBoost reply to reviews automatically?
No. Drafts are suggestions and a reply is posted only after you explicitly confirm it.
Can you delete a bad review for me?
No. Only Google can remove a review, and only if it breaks their policies. We explain how to report one.
Do I need to give you my Google password?
No. Connection uses Google sign-in. We never see your password and you can disconnect at any time.
