Reports and monitoring

A report turns scattered numbers into a story you can act on or share with a partner. Monitoring watches for changes between reports so you are not surprised.

AvailabilityReports and monitoring are Pro features that are launching. Snapshots, CSV export, share links and weekly or monthly schedules exist in the backend and tests, but need the production API online; performance and Search Console data also need Google sign-in and approval. Email delivery needs an email provider that is not configured yet.

What a report contains

A report is a list of sections. Each section has a title, rows of figures, the source of those figures, the date they are valid for and a flag showing whether the data was available. If it was not, the section says why instead of showing zeros. Typical sections are an overview of your score and open findings, reviews received and unanswered, profile performance such as searches and actions where Google provides them, Search Console clicks and impressions for your website, and the audit history.

Measured versus estimated

Figures that arrive from Google or from our own audit are measured. Anything we calculate, such as a score or a change since last month, is derived from those figures and is labelled that way. We do not project future traffic and we do not convert impressions into revenue.

Monitoring and schedules

Weekly or monthly reports can be scheduled for a business. Monitoring compares your latest data with earlier snapshots and records events worth looking at: a new review, a review still unanswered after several days, a changed profile field or a new high severity finding. In-app notifications exist; email notifications need an email provider that has not been configured, and the system reports that state honestly instead of claiming delivery. This is not a real-time alert service.

Printing and sharing

Reports are designed to print cleanly. You can create a read-only link to a saved snapshot for a colleague or client who has no account. The link is a long random token, only a hash of which is stored. It expires after a date you choose (thirty days by default, at most ninety), you can revoke it any time, and you can have a limited number active at once. The shared page shows the saved snapshot only, strips internal identifiers and email addresses, and is marked so search engines do not index it. On the Agency plan the link can carry your own business name, logo and accent colour. The shared report viewer explains clearly when a link is invalid, expired, or when the production API is not connected.

Limitations

  • Google data arrives with delays, and some periods may be missing.
  • Reports show what happened. They do not tell you why, and correlation is not cause.
  • A report is only as complete as the sources you have connected.
  • Sharing a link shares the snapshot with anyone who has it, so treat it like a document you would email.

Frequently asked questions

Can I send a report to a client?
Yes, once sharing is live. You create a read-only link to a saved snapshot with an expiry date, and you can revoke it.
How often are reports updated?
Reports are snapshots. A new one is created when you generate it or on a weekly or monthly schedule; there is no real-time dashboard.
Do reports include revenue figures?
No. We do not have your sales data and we do not estimate it.
