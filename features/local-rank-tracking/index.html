Local rank tracking: positions across a map grid

Your position in Google Maps changes with where the searcher stands. Rank tracking scans a grid of points around your business and records where you appear at each point.

AvailabilityRank tracking needs a licensed rank-data provider, and none has been connected yet. Until one is, scans answer with an explicit state that says the provider is not available. Nothing is simulated. It is a Pro feature that is launching.

Why a grid instead of one number

Local results are personal to location. A salon can be first for someone standing next to it and nowhere to be seen two kilometres away. A single rank number hides that. A grid scan places points in a square around your business, asks for the local results at each point and records your position there. The result is a map of strength: the points where you show up, and the gaps.

What you configure

You choose a keyword you already track, a grid of three by three, five by five or seven by seven points, and a radius in metres. Larger grids and wider radii give a fuller picture but use more of your plan's scan allowance. A scan can be run once or scheduled to repeat so you can see change over time.

What a result contains

  • Position per point. A number when your business appeared in the results, or empty when it did not. We never fill an empty point with a guess.
  • Average position. Calculated only from points where you were found, shown together with how many points that was.
  • Visibility. A simple summary of how much of the grid you appear in, so scans of the same size can be compared.
  • Provider and time. Each point records which provider returned it and when.
  • History and CSV. Past scans are kept so you can chart movement, and any scan can be exported as CSV.

Data source and honesty

Google does not offer a public API that returns Maps rankings by location, so any tool that shows them relies on a data provider or its own collection. LocalBoost uses a provider adapter so a licensed supplier with Indian coverage can be plugged in. We have not verified or signed with a vendor yet, so we do not state pricing, accuracy or coverage figures. When the adapter is not configured, the API responds that a rank provider is awaited. When a provider errors, the scan is stored as partial or failed, not as a success.

Try the idea for free

The rank grid simulator lets you explore how a grid looks. It is a learning aid with illustrative data and is clearly labelled as a simulation. It does not show your real rankings.

Limitations

  • Results from a provider are a sample from one moment and one kind of device. They are not what every customer sees.
  • Grids do not cover every street. The radius and number of points limit detail.
  • Personalised results, such as places a searcher has visited before, are not reproduced.
  • Rank tracking shows where you are. It does not change where you are; see our rank tracking guide for what usually moves positions, with no guarantees.

Frequently asked questions

Is the grid I see in the simulator my real ranking?
No. The simulator is a teaching tool with illustrative data. Real scans need a connected rank-data provider.
Do you guarantee a Maps position?
No. Nobody can. We report what was observed and leave the decisions to you.
Why is a grid point empty?
It means your business did not appear in the results returned for that point. We keep it empty rather than invent a position.
