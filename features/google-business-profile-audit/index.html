Google Business Profile audit

Your Business Profile is the first thing many customers see. The audit checks whether the basics are filled in, consistent and complete, and tells you what to fix first.

AvailabilityThe free audit works from the details you enter, with no Google connection. Reading your real profile automatically needs Google sign-in and Business Profile API approval, which is pending. That live version is part of the Pro launch.

What the audit checks

  • Name. Is it your real-world business name, without added keywords or locations? Keyword stuffing can get a profile suspended.
  • Primary category. Is the most specific category chosen? This is one of the strongest relevance signals you control.
  • Secondary categories. Are other genuine services listed?
  • Address and service area. Is the address complete, and is a service area set if you travel to customers?
  • Phone and website. Present, working and matching your website footer and other listings.
  • Hours. Regular hours set, plus special hours for festivals and holidays.
  • Description. Present and a sensible length, written for customers, with no promotional claims that break the rules.
  • Reviews. The number of reviews and how many are unanswered.
  • Name, address and phone consistency. Whether your details match between your profile and website.

Provided by you or verified

Every check records where its information came from. When you type details into the free audit, the finding says it is user provided, because we cannot confirm it matches Google. When a Google connection exists, the same check is marked verified because the data came from your account. When data could not be read, the check is marked unavailable and is left out of the score. This distinction matters, so we show it on every finding.

Why the audit does not score photos, posts or Q and A yet

Those areas are real parts of a good profile, but their data needs the live Google connection, and we only show a check when we can evidence it. They are not scored from guesses. When the connection exists, we plan to add checks for them, and we will say so in the changelog.

What you can do before Pro opens

  1. Run the free business audit and note the failed items.
  2. Work through the local SEO checklist.
  3. Read the Google Business Profile guide for step-by-step instructions on each field.

Limitations

  • We are not Google and cannot see what Google sees internally, such as suspensions or quality filters.
  • Edits you make in Google can take time to appear.
  • We do not make changes to your profile unless you ask for a specific write action and confirm it.
  • Categories and attributes available differ by country and business type.

Plan availability

The self-entered audit is free. Live profile audits with history and comparison are a Pro feature. See the pricing page and join the waitlist below.

Frequently asked questions

Do you need access to my Google account?
Not for the free audit. For the live version you sign in with Google and grant access that you can revoke at any time.
Will the audit edit my profile?
No. Audits only read. A write action, such as posting a review reply, requires your explicit confirmation.
Why is a check marked unavailable?
We could not read that information. Unavailable checks are excluded from the score instead of counted as failures.
