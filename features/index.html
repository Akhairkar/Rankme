LocalBoost features, with honest status

LocalBoost helps small businesses in India understand and improve how they appear in local search. This page lists every feature, what data it uses and whether it is live today.

What you can use today and what is launching

Some of LocalBoost is available now and some is being finished. The free tools run in your browser without an account: the free business audit, the local SEO checklist, generators for descriptions, services and review replies, and the rank grid simulator. The paid product, called Pro and Agency, adds saved history, live Google data, rank scans, review monitoring and reports. Those need the production API, Google approval for Business Profile access and a licensed rank-data provider. We have not finished those approvals, so we call the paid features launching and we invite you to join the waitlist instead of selling you something that does not work yet.

How to read the labels

Every feature page uses the same labels, so you can judge claims quickly.

  • Measured means we read the value directly from a page, from Google or from a provider.
  • Estimated means we calculated a summary or a planning figure from measured values. Scores are in this group.
  • You provide means the information came from what you typed. We mark it so it is never mistaken for verified data.
  • Needs approval means the step depends on something outside our control, such as Google API access or a rank-data licence.

Principles behind every feature

  1. No ranking guarantees. Rankings depend on factors nobody outside Google can see. We report observations and recommendations only.
  2. Missing data is shown as missing. An unavailable check is left out, not scored as a failure, and an empty grid point stays empty.
  3. Read-only first. Google access starts with read permissions where possible, and anything that writes to your profile needs an explicit confirmation.
  4. You stay in control. You can export your data, disconnect Google and delete your account. See data export and deletion.
  5. Independent product. LocalBoost is not affiliated with Google.

Plans in one paragraph

Free covers the browser tools and a limited number of audits. Pro is aimed at a single business owner who wants rank scans, review monitoring, reports and competitors. Agency raises the limits for several businesses. Exact prices and limits are on the pricing page, which is the source of truth, and prices are shown excluding GST.
