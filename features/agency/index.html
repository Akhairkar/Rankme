Feature

LocalBoost for agencies and multi-location businesses

If you look after several businesses, you need higher limits and tidy reporting. The Agency plan is designed for that, and this page is plain about which parts exist and which do not.

AvailabilityThe Agency plan is launching, not live. Team roles, invitations, audit logs and white-label share links exist in the backend code and its tests, but they have not run against production, and the dashboard screens for them may not be finished. Join the waitlist.

Who the Agency plan is for

Freelancers and small agencies handling the local presence of a few to a few dozen clients, and owners of a small chain who want each branch watched separately. It is not an enterprise product and does not claim to be.

What the plan includes by design

The plan definitions in the billing code give Agency higher allowances than Pro: more businesses and locations, more tracked keywords, more audits per month, more rank scans, more competitors and a larger crawl page limit. We do not repeat exact numbers here because limits can change before launch; the pricing page is the source of truth for prices and limits. Annual billing is available, and prices are shown excluding GST, with GST added at checkout and shown on the invoice.

We call these built in the backend because they have been tested against a local database with mocked services only. None has run in production, and each depends on the production API going online.

What does not exist yet

There is no bulk import, no separate client portal beyond the read-only role, and no priority support or service-level agreement. White-label applies to shared report links only: the LocalBoost name is still part of the product and the dashboard. We would rather say this clearly now than have you plan a client onboarding around features that are not there.

How agencies can work today

Use the free tools for first conversations: the free audit and competitor comparison give a prospect something concrete. Keep your own checklist per client using the local SEO checklist. When Pro and Agency open, saved audits and reports will replace manual notes.

Working with client Google accounts

For a client's Business Profile, the client should add you as a manager in Google, or you should sign in with a Google account that already has access. Never ask a client for their password. LocalBoost uses Google sign-in only, which keeps passwords out of our hands. See team and permissions for how roles work.

Limitations

  • No ranking guarantees, so avoid promising your clients any.
  • Some data needs Google approval and a rank provider, which are outstanding.
  • Reporting is a snapshot, not a live dashboard.

How a typical agency workflow will look

You create a workspace, add each client business, and invite a colleague as manager so they can run audits and scans without being able to change billing or the team. For a client who only wants to look, you add them as a client assigned to their own business, which gives read-only access to that business and nothing else. You generate a monthly report per business, review it, then share it as a link that expires on a date you choose, between one and ninety days. You can revoke the link earlier. Because every account uses Google sign-in, nobody shares a password.

Frequently asked questions

Can my team log in to the same account?
The backend supports workspaces with roles and invitations, and each person signs in with their own Google account. The screens for managing a team are still being finished, so it is not available to use yet.
Do you offer white-label reports?
Shared report links can carry your own name, logo and accent colour on the Agency plan. That is built in the backend but not yet live. Other parts of the product keep the LocalBoost name.
When does the Agency plan launch?
No date is fixed because it depends on Google approval, a rank provider and payment account approval. Join the waitlist for updates.
