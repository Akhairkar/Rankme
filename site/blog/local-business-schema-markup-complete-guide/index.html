Complete LocalBusiness JSON-LD Schema Guide for 2026

9 min read • August 10, 2026 • By LocalBoost Research Team

Structured data markup is the technical bridge connecting your business website directly to Google's Knowledge Graph. Implementing clean JSON-LD schema confirms your physical authenticity.

1. The Fundamental LocalBusiness Schema Block

Always declare exact latitude and longitude coordinates matching your Google Maps pin, alongside exact address components (streetAddress, addressLocality, postalCode, addressCountry).

2. Detailed Opening Hours Specification

Declare your precise opening and closing times for each day of the week using openingHoursSpecification to prevent Google from displaying conflicting hours.

3. Linking Your Official Place ID & Social Citations

Use the hasMap and sameAs properties to cross-reference your official Google Maps URL, Justdial listing, and social profiles.
