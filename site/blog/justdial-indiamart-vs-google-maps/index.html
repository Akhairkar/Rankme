Google Business Profile vs. Justdial & IndiaMART: The Real ROI Comparison

11 min read • July 30, 2026 • By LocalBoost Research Team

Indian small business owners spend lakhs annually on directory packages. Comparing customer intent, inquiry quality, and long-term ownership reveals where your marketing focus belongs.

1. Shared Leads vs. Direct Exclusive Inquiries

Directory portals frequently sell a single customer lead to 4 to 8 competing vendors simultaneously, triggering an aggressive price race to the bottom. In contrast, a customer calling directly from Google Maps calls exclusively your business.

2. Recurring Directory Fees vs. Permanent Digital Equity

When you stop paying directory subscription fees, your listing visibility drops to zero immediately. In contrast, building 150 verified reviews and high local 3-pack rank on Google Maps creates permanent digital equity that continues generating free leads for years.

3. High Commercial Intent on Google Maps

Customers searching 'near me' on Google Maps have immediate buying intent and are located within your physical radius. 76% of local mobile searches result in a physical store visit or phone call within 24 hours.
