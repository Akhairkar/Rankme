# KEYWORD MAP — Local Business Growth Platform (India-focused)

This document defines the keyword architecture for the platform's SEO/content layer. No pages are built in this session. No search volumes are claimed — priority is directional (High/Medium/Low) based on topical relevance and intent, not verified volume data.

---

## 1. Pillar Clusters

1. **Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup & Optimization**
2. **Google Maps Ranking & Local Visibility**
3. **Local SEO Fundamentals**
4. **Google Reviews & Reputation**
5. **Business-Type + Google Maps (vertical pages)**
6. **City + Local SEO (geo pages)**
7. **Getting Customers From Google (conversion/growth)**

Each pillar maps to one pillar page, with cluster pages linking back to it (hub-and-spoke model — no doorway pages, no near-duplicate city/business permutation pages without unique content).

---

## 9. Cannibalization Controls (Summary)

- One canonical pillar page per topic; cluster/guide pages link up to the pillar, never compete for the same primary keyword.
- Business-type pages and city pages are structurally separate; combined pages only created with genuinely unique content and real intent evidence.
- Hindi/Hinglish variants are treated as language-targeted versions of the same cluster (hreflang / clear language labeling), not separate keyword-stuffed pages.
- No page is created solely to "capture" a keyword variant without adding distinct value — avoids doorway-page patterns per Session 01/02 constraints.
