How it works

Six steps from “where do I start?” to a report you can trust

With the free tools you do steps 3 and 4 yourself using guided checklists. With Pro, steps 1 to 6 are connected.

Add your business

Name, category, city and website. One business on Pro; several on Agency.

Connect your data

Sign in with Google and approve read-only access to your Business Profile. You can disconnect at any time.

Audit

LocalBoost checks your profile, website and reviews, and records what it found with its source.

Fix

Work through an ordered action list. Nothing is changed on your profile unless you confirm it.

Track

Follow reviews, rankings for chosen keywords and competitors as real measurements come in.

Report

Share a monthly report that shows what changed, with evidence, and what is still open.

Why you can trust the reports

We show our working

A report is only useful if you can check it. These are the rules every LocalBoost report follows.

Real data only

If a measurement is not available, the report says “awaiting data”. We never fill gaps with made-up numbers.

Evidence and source on every finding

Each finding says what we saw, where we saw it and when, so you can verify it.

Simulations are labelled

Practice tools and estimates are marked as simulated or estimated, never mixed in with measurements.

No ranking guarantees

Nobody outside Google controls rankings. We tell you what to improve, not where you will rank.

Independent of Google

LocalBoost is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. We use Google’s official sign-in and ask for permission first.

Limits stated plainly

A rank check is a snapshot, results differ by location and device. Read the limitations.
